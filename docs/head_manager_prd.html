Executive summary and product direction

When a C-Level Executive or Head Manager asks an operational or strategic question, the assistant returns auditable, live, grounded business facts directly from 4S Plus ERP & CRM—without hallucinations, manual query writing, or robotic AI gibberish.

SECTION 02
Problem hypotheses and jobs to be done
H1 · ERP DATA FRAGMENTATION
Enterprise Systems Are Too Complex
4S Plus ERP contains hundreds of tables. A Head Manager cannot write T-SQL queries or wait hours for business analysts to export spreadsheets during crucial meetings.
H2 · GENERIC LLM HALLUCINATIONS
Public AI Tools Are Disconnected
Generic AI models (ChatGPT, Claude) have zero visibility into live customer balances, hallucinate numbers, and speak in robotic, translated Modern Standard Arabic.
H3 · DATA PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE
Companies Cannot Leak Financials
Egyptian enterprises refuse to upload confidential customer debts, payroll penalties, and sales margins into unvetted public cloud training datasets.
H4 · DECISION LATENCY
Managers Need Answers in Seconds
Decision makers need direct summaries: What is total debt? Who owes the most? How many tickets are blocked? Response time must be sub-second.
2.2 Jobs to be Done (JTBD)
Who (Persona) Job the Product Helps Complete Proof of Value
Head Manager / CEO Get instant, accurate oversight over enterprise customer debt, cash flow, and team health. Exact figures matching general ledger in < 1 second, formatted in Egyptian Arabic.
Finance Director Identify top debtor accounts exceeding credit limits before signing off on purchase orders. Ranked list of top 5 indebted clients with exact outstanding balances from CustomerBalance.
Operations / CRM Head Monitor open customer complaints and department backlogs before client churn occurs. Breakdown of open CRM tickets by department with zero manual ticket filtering.
HR Director Audit disciplinary penalties and employee headcounts across company branches. Headcount distribution across 9 departments and live deduction records from HR_Employee_CR_Penalties.

SECTION 03
Enterprise data catalog & 4S ERP database mapping

The assistant does not guess or generate speculative SQL. It interfaces directly with verified business entities in the 4S Plus MS SQL Server database (ERPWithCRM), mapped to dedicated executive analysis endpoints.

Business Domain 4S SQL Server Tables Executive Metric & Query Capability
Finance & Receivables dbo.CustomerBalance
dbo.Customer		 Total market receivables (149.2M SAR), Top 5 indebted clients, individual debtor balances, client master directory (32,416 clients).
Sales & Orders dbo.IMSSalesOrder
dbo.IMSSalesOrderDetail		 Recent sales orders, order status distribution, sales velocity, order value aggregations.
CRM & Customer Care dbo.ITR_CRM_Tickets
dbo.ITR_CRM_Lead		 Open ticket backlog by department, complaint status, customer care resolution rates, lead pipeline (55 active leads).
HR & Workforce dbo.Department
dbo.Employee
dbo.HR_Employee_CR_Penalties		 Headcount across 9 departments (481 employees), active team rosters, disciplinary penalty audits and salary deduction logs.
Zero-Trust Database Invariant: The database backend operates under an absolute SELECT constraint. The user account crm_app_user has no privileges to alter tables, drop records, or modify balances. All queries are parameterized to guarantee complete immunity against SQL injection.
DATABASE HOST
4splus-ai.ddns.net
Port 1433, encrypted TLS, authenticated via dedicated service account.
ODBC DRIVER
ODBC Driver 17
High-performance native C++ connection pooling with pyodbc.
TEST FALLBACK
SQLite Sandbox
Zero-network unit test runner ensuring 100% CI/CD stability.

SECTION 04
Language & Executive Tone: Egyptian Business Colloquial

The Head Manager AI Assistant completely abandons the generic, robotic persona of conventional chatbots. Responses are articulated in authentic Egyptian Business Colloquial Arabic (اللهجة المصرية العامية التنفيذية)—natural, authoritative, respectful, and numbers-first.

RULE 1 · NUMBERS IN THE FIRST LINE
An executive wants the bottom line immediately. State the total, count, or primary metric in the opening sentence before giving details.
RULE 2 · ZERO ROBOTIC AI PHRASES
Banned phrases: "بصفتي نموذج ذكاء اصطناعي", "يسعدني مساعدتك اليوم", "بناءً على قاعدة البيانات". The assistant speaks as a seasoned executive peer.
RULE 3 · NATURAL CODE-SWITCHING
Seamlessly blends natural Egyptian terms ("مسجلين بالسيستم", "عليهم مديونية", "إجمالي المديونيات") with industry terms (CRM, ERP, Tickets).
RULE 4 · CRISP VISUAL FORMATTING
Uses numbered bullet points and highlighted bold figures so the executive can scan a multi-million balance breakdown in under 3 seconds.

SECTION 05
Information architecture & Executive UI

The Executive UI is designed for high-stress boardroom and mobile operations: zero visual distraction, immediate status cues, and an ergonomic persistent Dark Mode engine.

THEME ENGINE
Dark / Light System
CSS light-dark() tokens, auto-detects OS preference, manual toggle (☀️ / 🌙).
ZERO-FOUC
Inline Head Script
0ms flash of unstyled content on page load, synced across tabs via localStorage.
LATENCY INDICATOR
Live Telemetry
Every response displays exact SQL execution and synthesis time in milliseconds.
EXECUTIVE INTERFACE ANATOMY
UI Component Design Specification Executive Function
Topbar Clean branding, green live connection badge, Theme toggle. Confirms 4S SQL Server connection status at a glance.
Executive Chips Horizontal carousel with pre-calculated core queries. One-tap retrieval of receivables, debtors, HR penalties, tickets.
Chat Stream High contrast speech bubbles, clear speaker labels. Formatted Arabic typography with formatted numeric tables.
Input Bar Autosizing text box with submit button and hotkey (Enter). Supports typing complex questions or pasting account codes.
UI Distraction Removal: The redundant "View source project" button was permanently excised from the sidebar to maintain a strictly professional, production-grade appearance during executive presentations.
